# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Malaysian parents, roughly 30 to 50, evaluating online maths tuition for a child from Standard 1 through Form 5, plus IGCSE. Most visitors arrive on mobile and want a clear, low-pressure answer to whether one-to-one tutoring is worth trying.

## Product Purpose

Just Math Malaysia is a solo online maths tuition service taught by Mr Kong. The site exists to make the offer clear, establish why one tutor across primary and secondary levels is credible, explain price and process without sales pressure, and move a parent into a WhatsApp message for a free 30-minute assessment.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, serious independent practitioner. The voice is first-person singular, specific, plain, and reassuring. It should feel closer to a careful accountant or architect than to an edtech startup or tuition centre.

## Anti-references

Avoid edtech SaaS grammar: gradient heroes, floating dashboard mockups, abstract 3D shapes, platform language, hype metrics, and product-led visuals. Avoid tuition-centre grammar: bright primary colours, cartoon mascots, clip-art pencils, graduation caps, exclamation marks, smiling stock children, testimonials placeholders, and achievement language such as excellence, mastery, potential, journey, or transform.

## Design Principles

Make WhatsApp unmistakable. Green is reserved for the repeated conversion action, and every green control must open WhatsApp.

Let numbers carry confidence. Years teaching, years online, student count, prices, session lengths, and assessment timing should be readable, concrete, and visually important.

Respect the copy. The page uses approved final copy; do not shorten, rewrite, invent claims, add testimonials, or create new offers.

Show the working. The layout should make the underlying argument visible: where school measurement gaps happen, how a session works, what each level needs, and what parents get after the assessment.

Stay serious and mobile-first. The site should feel calm, typographic, and practical on a phone, with desktop treated as an expansion rather than the primary canvas.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain readable contrast, visible focus, 44px touch targets, semantic headings, keyboard-accessible FAQ behavior, reduced-motion handling, responsive reflow, and language-aware copy for English and Bahasa Melayu references. Do not rely on colour alone to distinguish WhatsApp, pricing, or progress states.
