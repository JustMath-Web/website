## 1. The five sites

### 1.1 spmmath.my — the closest archetype
**Model:** solo tutor, all online, SPM maths only.
**Claims:** "experienced teacher with over 26 years of teaching experience"; opens on failure
statistics — "1 in 4 students do not pass".
**Price:** shown — "RM60 per month".
**CTA:** phone/WhatsApp — "Contact us at 011-27188183 via phone or WhatsApp to secure your spot."
**Proof:** general assurances ("consistently achieve excellent results") and parent testimonials; no
named students, no mark distributions.
**Urgency:** heavy — "limited to just 50 students", "SPM 2026 is approaching fast", a claimed "40%
higher" score for early starters.
**Visual:** text-heavy and information-first; no mascot, no strong colour identity.

> **This is the single most important row in the scan.** It is structurally the same business as Just
> Math — one experienced teacher, online, maths — and it competes on **fear plus scarcity**. Whatever
> Just Math does, it cannot win by being a calmer version of the same pitch; it has to make the
> opposite argument the visible one.

### 1.2 tavis.live — the platform

## 2. Conventions of the category

What nearly all of them do:

| Convention | Seen at | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scale as the proof** | hometuition (32500+), golearn (12000+), algonova (1,000,000+) | Bigger number = more trustworthy |
| **Free trial as the entry** | golearn (30-min trial), algonova (Master Class), nakTuition | The free first session is now table stakes, not a differentiator |
| **WhatsApp as the real conversion** | spmmath, tavis, golearn | Even platform funnels fall back to WhatsApp |
| **Price anchored low, or deferred** | spmmath RM60/mo, tavis RM99/mo, algonova "from RM48" | Cheapness is the common axis of competition |
| **Result claims without denominators** | spmmath, tavis, golearn | "Excellent results", "5/5", "Proven improvements" |
| **Blue trust palette** | tavis, golearn, algonova | Blue/purple tech-trust is the category default |
| **Children in the imagery** | algonova, golearn, tavis | Photography of kids learning; the parent is rarely addressed directly |
| **Fear or urgency framing** | spmmath (hardest), golearn, tavis | Failure stats, limited seats, intake deadlines |

What almost none of them do:

- **Address the parent as the reader.** Copy is about the child and to the child.
- **Show a real, named individual teacher as the product.** Even spmmath's solo tutor is unnamed on the page.
- **Publish the actual mechanism** — what happens in a session, minute to minute.
- **Give a number with its denominator**, or admit a limit ("I only take N students").
- **Say no.** Nobody declines a booking, scopes themselves out, or names what they don't teach.

## 3. The named slop trap

> **The category sells reassurance through scale and urgency: a big tutor count or a big student
> count, a low monthly price, an exam deadline, and a free trial — wrapped in blue, illustrated with
> stock children, and addressed past the parent to the child.**

Its tired phrases, appended to CS-60's banned list for this project:

`excellent results` · `proven results` · `top tutors` · `qualified & experienced` · `unlock/unleash
potential` · `achieve your dreams` · `score A+` · `limited seats` · `secure your spot` · `don't miss
out` · `book now` · `#1 / No. 1 / best in Malaysia` · `happy students` · `satisfied parents` ·
`comprehensive` · `holistic learning` · `learning journey`

## 4. The differentiation space this leaves open

1. **Calm as the entire pitch.** The closest competitor opens on "1 in 4 students do not pass" and
   closes on "limited to just 50 students". The unoccupied position is the practitioner who says
   *"usually replies the same day"* and never once creates pressure.
2. **The parent as the reader.** Nobody writes to the person actually paying and worrying.
3. **Specific numbers with denominators.** In a field of "excellent results", "34 out of 40" is a
   different kind of claim — and it is checkable.
4. **Naming the limit.** "500+ students" is everywhere; *"the 500 number, and why it is small on
   purpose"* inverts the category's own boast.
5. **Publishing the mechanism.** No competitor explains what a session actually looks like, or when
   the school system does and does not measure a child's maths.
6. **Being the same person every week.** Every marketplace and platform is structurally unable to
   promise this. It is the one claim they cannot copy.

## 5. What the design inherits and rejects

| From | Inherit | Reject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| spmmath.my | Transparent pricing on the page; a solo practitioner's credibility | Fear opener, scarcity, undenominated result claims |
| tavis.live | WhatsApp as a first-class conversion path | Blue/purple tech palette, decorative shapes, testimonial galleries |
| hometuitionmalaysia.com | Honest tiered price table | Scale-as-proof, placeholder imagery, "GET A TUTOR NOW" volume |
| golearn.com.my | Level-by-level structure (primary → IGCSE) | Emoji iconography, star ratings, "Hot Question", intake urgency |
| algonova.com | Curriculum alignment stated plainly (KSSR/KSSM) | Global scale claims, stock lifestyle photography of children |
