## Q1 · Register & tone the audience expects — and what reads as untrustworthy

Parents buying tuition for a child aged 5–17 expect plain competence and a clear price
`[client: just-math-design-brief.md]`. The category speaks in encouragement and urgency
`[scan: spmmath.my, tavis.live]`.

What reads as **untrustworthy in this sector specifically**:

- Undenominated success claims — "consistently achieve excellent results" `[scan: spmmath.my]`,
  "5/5 Average Rating" `[scan: golearn.com.my]`. Parents have seen these on every site.
- Scarcity around a child's education — "limited to just 50 students", "secure your spot"
  `[scan: spmmath.my]`.
- Fear as the opener — "1 in 4 students do not pass" `[scan: spmmath.my]`.
- Anonymity: a "qualified tutor" with no name and no face, when the entire service is one person's
  time `[scan: hometuitionmalaysia.com, golearn.com.my]`.

**Implication:** the register is a professional practice, not a school and not a startup — closer to
an accountant's or an architect's site `[client: brief]`. First person singular throughout, because
there is genuinely one person `[client: page copy]`.

## Q2 · Proof devices that actually convert here

Ranked for a solo practitioner:

1. **A named, visible person with a verifiable history.** 24 years teaching, 10 online
   `[client: page copy]`. No competitor in the scan names their teacher on the landing page.
2. **Specific numbers with denominators.** "34 out of 40" rather than "excellent results"
   `[client: brand brief]` — a direct inversion of the category norm `[scan: all five]`.
3. **Transparent, complete pricing.** Table stakes here: spmmath (RM60/mo), tavis (RM99/mo),
   hometuition (RM30–100/hr) all publish `[scan]`. Hiding price would be the anomaly.
4. **The free assessment.** Universal in the category `[scan: golearn 30-min trial, algonova Master
   Class]` — necessary to match, useless as a differentiator.
5. **A real artefact.** The monthly written progress report `[client: brand brief]`. Nothing
   comparable appears anywhere in the scan.
6. **Naming a limit.** Declining work, or capping student numbers, reads as confidence
   `[client: page copy, "why it is small on purpose"]`.

**Not a proof device here:** logo walls, accreditation badges, star ratings, testimonial galleries.
The brief rules out testimonials entirely `[client: brief]` and the design ships no section for them.

## Q3 · Imagery reality

- **Genuine:** a real tutor at a real desk, a whiteboard mid-working, an actual marked script or
  report page `[client: brief — "a good accountant or architect's site"]`.
- **The stock cliché of it:** children smiling at laptops, diverse group study scenes, lifestyle
  home-learning photography `[scan: algonova.com, golearn.com.my, tavis.live]`.
- **What the client can realistically supply:** **one photograph of Mr Kong, and nothing else**
  `[client: brief — "There is exactly one photograph and no logo yet"]`.

**Implication — this is the constraint the whole design is built around.** Art direction must work
with a supply of exactly one image. Everywhere else the page is typographic, and where a competitor
puts a hero image this brand puts a real artefact. Stock photography of children would actively
damage a page whose entire claim is that one named person teaches every session `[client: brief]`.

## Q4 · The buyer's real objections and decision timeline

**Timeline:** considered, not impulse — typically triggered by a specific event (a bad exam result,
a new school year, Form 4 starting, Additional Mathematics arriving) `[client: page copy]`. Days to
weeks, with a parent–child conversation in the middle. `[unknown — ask the client: typical time from
first WhatsApp message to first paid session]`

**Real objections, in the order parents raise them** `[client: page copy FAQ — 13 items]`:

1. Can one tutor genuinely teach both a seven-year-old and an Add Maths student?
2. Is online actually as good as in person?
3. Will my child hold attention online for an hour?
4. Does this fit around school and existing tuition?
5. What exactly am I paying for, and when?
6. What happens if we stop?

**Contact channel follows directly:** WhatsApp, because that is how Malaysian parents already
transact and because it lets them ask one question without committing `[client: brief — every
conversion is a WhatsApp message]`. The whole category confirms it, even the platforms
`[scan: tavis.live "Whatsapp Now", spmmath.my, golearn.com.my]`. Mobile-first is not a preference
here; it is the medium.

## Q5 · Regulatory, claims and licensing sensitivity

- **Claims about exam outcomes** are the sensitive area. Guaranteeing grades is both a credibility
  risk and a claim no honest tutor can make; the category's undenominated results language sits
  right at that line `[scan: spmmath.my, tavis.live]`. Design rule: no statistic without its
  denominator, no invented figures `[client: brand brief]`.
- **Syllabus naming** — KSSR, KSSM, SPM, IGCSE — must be used accurately; these are official
  Malaysian curriculum terms `[client: page copy]`.
- **Assessment policy is live and dated.** The Learning Matrix (Year 4 from October 2026, Form 3
  from 2027) is current policy `[client: page copy]`; it will date and needs a review owner.
- **Minors' data and imagery.** No student photographs, no named children, no testimonials from
  children — the design has none `[client: brief]`.
- **PDPA:** WhatsApp is the only data channel; there is no form, no account, no email capture, so the
  surface area is minimal `[client: brief]`. `[unknown — ask the client: any privacy notice required
  for the WhatsApp channel]`
- **WhatsApp trademark:** nominative use of the mark and colour needs a guidelines check
  (`ASSETS.md` §4).

## Q6 · The sector slop trap

**The trap** (from `MARKET-SCAN.md` §3): *the category sells reassurance through scale and urgency —
a big tutor count or student count, a low monthly price, an exam deadline, a free trial — wrapped in
blue, illustrated with stock children, and addressed past the parent to the child.*

**Banned phrases appended to CS-60 for this project:** `excellent results` · `proven results` ·
`top tutors` · `qualified & experienced` · `unlock/unleash potential` · `achieve your dreams` ·
`score A+` · `limited seats` · `secure your spot` · `don't miss out` · `book now` ·
`#1 / No. 1 / best in Malaysia` · `happy students` · `satisfied parents` · `comprehensive` ·
`holistic learning` · `learning journey`

**Differentiation space it leaves open:** calm instead of urgency; the parent as the reader; numbers
with denominators; naming a limit; publishing the mechanism; and being the same person every week —
the one claim a marketplace structurally cannot make.

## Derived discovery questions

Sector-specific, generated from the profile. These extend the discovery Q&A; they do not replace it.

1. **The closest competitor is another solo tutor with 26 years' experience who opens on "1 in 4
   students do not pass" and caps at 50 students.** You have 24 years and refuse to use fear. If a
   parent compares the two pages side by side, what do you want them to conclude?
2. Every competitor leads with scale or price. **What can you claim that a marketplace with 32,500
   tutors structurally cannot?** (Working hypothesis: the same person, every week, who has seen the
   same child across several years.)
3. You cap student numbers deliberately. **What is the actual number, and what happens when you are
   full?** The page currently implies a limit without stating the mechanism.
4. The category gives a free trial as a sales device. **What makes your 30 minutes different from a
   trial lesson** — and are you willing to tell a parent their child does not need tutoring?
5. The Learning Matrix dates (Oct 2026, 2027) are load-bearing on the page and will date.
   **Who reviews them, and when?**
6. **Is the photograph coming?** If no portrait can be supplied, the About section needs a
   typographic rebuild — that is a design decision, not a content delay.
7. `[unknown]` items above need answers: time from first message to first session, and whether the
   WhatsApp channel needs a privacy notice.
