Opens every section on the landing page and every block in a report.

```jsx
<SectionHeading eyebrow="How it works" title="Three steps, no forms to fill in." lead="You message me, we try one lesson, then you decide." />
```

Eyebrow is uppercase 12px wide-tracked sans; title is serif; lead is grey sans at 19px. Keep titles to one sentence with a full stop.
